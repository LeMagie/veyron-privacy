01 / OVERVIEW

Introduction

Welcome to Veyron. This Privacy Policy explains how the operator of Veyron processes personal information when you interact with our Discord bot and AI-powered support features.

This policy applies to Veyron's own processing. Discord and xAI also process information under their respective terms and privacy notices. This document does not replace those notices.

03 / DATA

Information processed

Discord account and interaction information

  • Your Discord user ID, used to associate your privacy choice with your account.
  • Your Discord username or display name, where it is shown alongside your message in the server.
  • The messages and context you choose to submit to the AI support feature.

Consent records

Veyron records your Discord user ID, consent status, the date and time of your decision, and the policy version associated with that decision. These records are used to check whether AI processing is permitted.

Technical information

Veyron may process limited technical information needed to operate the bot, diagnose errors, prevent abuse, and protect the service. The operator should document any additional logs actually enabled by the bot or its hosting provider.

04 / PURPOSES

Why we process information

  • To receive and relay your support request to the AI provider.
  • To return the generated answer in the relevant Discord channel.
  • To record and verify your AI consent choice.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of Veyron and prevent misuse.
  • To respond to privacy requests and comply with legal obligations.

Legal bases

Where required, AI processing is based on your consent. Limited security, abuse-prevention, and service-operation processing may rely on the operator's legitimate interests, after balancing those interests against your rights. Processing may also be required to comply with legal obligations.

You may withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal does not make processing carried out before withdrawal unlawful.

05 / THIRD-PARTY AI

AI provider: xAI (Grok)

Veyron uses the xAI API to process prompts and generate AI responses. When you use the AI feature, the message you submit and the contextual information included by the bot are transmitted to xAI. The API provider processes this information to deliver the requested response and under its applicable terms.

xAI API retention information

According to xAI's API security documentation, API requests and responses are stored by default for 30 days for auditing in cases of suspected abuse or misuse. xAI states that API inputs and outputs are not used to train its models without explicit permission, and that the default retained data is automatically deleted after 30 days, subject to applicable exceptions and product-specific settings.

Read xAI's API security documentation ↗

This retention is controlled by xAI, not by Veyron. Veyron does not control xAI's internal systems or independently delete API-side records.

AI-generated answers may be inaccurate, incomplete, or misleading. Do not rely on them as professional, legal, medical, financial, or emergency advice.

06 / DISCORD VISIBILITY

Messages may be visible in Discord

Veyron acts as a relay between your Discord interaction and the xAI API. Veyron does not intentionally keep a separate database or archive of the messages sent to Grok.

However, the bot displays the submitted message and/or the AI interaction in the relevant Discord server channel together with your Discord name. Members who have permission to view that channel may therefore see the content. The message may also remain available in Discord according to Discord's own systems, settings, and retention practices.

Veyron cannot control screenshots, copies, or other records made by server members, nor Discord's independent processing of messages.

Please only submit information that you are comfortable sharing with the members who can access the channel.

07 / DISCLOSURE

Sharing and international transfers

Veyron does not sell personal information. Information is disclosed only as needed to operate the service:

  • Discord: to provide the server, channel, account, and message functionality.
  • xAI: to process the AI prompt and generate a response.
  • Hosting providers: to run the bot and host this privacy website, where applicable.

These providers may process data in countries outside the European Economic Area. The default xAI API endpoint does not, by itself, guarantee that processing takes place in a specific region. Where GDPR international-transfer rules apply, the operator must ensure an appropriate transfer mechanism and safeguards are in place before transferring personal data.

For more information, consult the providers' own privacy and data-protection documentation: Discord Privacy Policy and xAI Privacy Policy.

09 / YOUR CHOICE

Consent and withdrawal

Before using AI features that require consent, you must accept the applicable version of the AI Privacy Policy. Veyron stores the version and timestamp associated with your choice.

You can review your status or withdraw your consent through the /privacy command on Discord.

After withdrawal, Veyron must stop sending new requests that rely on that consent. Withdrawal does not automatically remove messages already posted in Discord or records already held by xAI. Those must be addressed through the relevant provider or platform procedures where deletion is available.

If the policy changes in a way that materially affects the processing or purposes covered by consent, Veyron may ask you to review and accept the new version before using the affected AI feature again.

10 / GDPR RIGHTS

Your privacy rights

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access personal information concerning you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request erasure of information.
  • Request restriction of processing.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Receive certain information in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

These rights are not absolute and may be subject to legal conditions or exceptions. To make a request, email VeyronUserService@outlook.fr. Please describe your request and provide enough information to identify the relevant Discord account. Do not send your password or authentication token.

If you are in France, you may also contact the CNIL: www.cnil.fr.

11 / SECURITY

Security

The operator takes reasonable steps to protect Veyron's stored consent records and service credentials. Access should be limited to people who need it to operate the service. API keys and bot tokens must be kept secret and must never be posted in Discord or included in public website code.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. If a personal data breach occurs, the operator will assess it and take any steps required by applicable law.

12 / UPDATES

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Veyron's features, providers, or data practices change. Each published version should have a version number and an effective date. Previous versions should remain available so users can identify the text associated with their consent.

Where a change requires renewed consent, Veyron will request it before continuing the affected processing.
